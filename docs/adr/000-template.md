# ADR 000 — Template

> **Status:** Accepted

## Context

What is the issue we are facing? What forces are at play — technical,
organizational, schedule? What constraints must the decision respect?

## Decision

What is the change we are making? State it precisely — the architecture,
pattern, or convention being adopted.

## Consequences

What follows from this decision?

- Positive: what does this enable or improve?
- Negative: what does this cost or constrain?
- Neutral: what side effects are worth noting?

## Alternatives Considered

What other options were evaluated? Why were they rejected? Briefly state each
alternative and the key reason it lost.
